What Exactly Is an Oodie Hoodie?

The original Oodie is an Australian-brand oversized hoodie blanket that has sold to over 4 million customers worldwide. It is made from the company’s proprietary ToastyTek fleece, which is a double-layered fabric combining an ultra-soft plush outer with a sherpa-lined interior. The result is genuinely softer and denser than the standard sherpa fleece you find on most competitors.

The brand offers licensed designs including Disney, BT21, Harry Potter, and dozens of original patterns. It is genuinely a premium product — but at roughly $100 USD (or £54 GBP), it is a significant investment for what is essentially a very well-made blanket with sleeves.

Why are oodies so comfy? The secret lies in three things: the oversized cut that traps warm air around your body, the double-layer ToastyTek fleece that provides insulation without feeling heavy, and the generous hood that keeps your neck and head warm. Together, these features create a microclimate effect — your body heat gets trapped in the air pockets between the fleece layers, and the loose fit means you never feel restricted. It is like wrapping yourself in a warm hug that you can walk around in.

The Contenders: Every Major Oodie Hoodie Alternative

1. Primark Snuddie — The Most Talked-About Oodie Dupe

Price: ~£16 (~$20 USD)

When people ask “What is the Primark version of an Oodie?” the answer is the Snuddie. At less than one-third the price of the original Oodie, the Primark Snuddie has generated enormous buzz — and for good reason.

The Snuddie uses a standard sherpa fleece construction rather than a proprietary fabric. The outer layer is a smooth polyester plush, and the interior is lined with a sherpa-style fleece. Side by side with the Oodie, you will notice the Snuddie is slightly thinner and the fabric is not quite as silky to the touch. The difference is real, but here is the thing: it is not as dramatic as you might expect for an $80 price gap.

What the Snuddie does well:

  • The fit is very similar — oversized, long sleeves, generous hood
  • Warmth is adequate for typical indoor use and short outdoor trips
  • Multiple color options, though no licensed character designs
  • Machine washable and holds up reasonably after washing

Where it falls short:

  • The fleece pills faster than the Oodie after 5–10 washes
  • The sherpa lining is thinner, so it is not quite as warm on very cold nights
  • Sizing runs slightly smaller than the original Oodie hoodie for adults

For $20, the Primark Snuddie delivers roughly 75–80% of the Oodie experience. If you just want something cozy for Netflix nights and you are not obsessed with ultra-premium fabric, it is a legitimate option.

2. Amazon Oodie Hoodie Alternatives — The Middle Ground

Price range: $25–$55 USD

If you search “oodie hoodie Amazon” you will find dozens of brands offering oversized hoodie blankets in the $25–$55 range. Brands like The Comfy, Comfyz, and various Amazon private labels compete aggressively here.

These mid-tier alternatives typically use a blend of polyester fleece and sherpa that is slightly better quality than the Primark Snuddie but not as refined as the original Oodie’s ToastyTek. The best options in this range tend to have:

  • Denser sherpa lining than the Snuddie
  • Better stitching quality and reinforced seams

The catch: Quality on Amazon is wildly inconsistent. You can end up with something that rivals the Oodie, or you can get a thin polyester shell that barely qualifies as a blanket. Reading reviews carefully is essential, and I recommend filtering for verified purchases with photos.

For an oodie hoodie blanket in this range, you are paying for convenience (fast Prime shipping) and a moderate quality bump over the cheapest options.

5 Things to Watch Out for When Buying an Oodie Hoodie Dupe

  1. Check the weight. A good oversized hoodie blanket should weigh at least 1.5–2 lbs. If the listing does not specify weight, read the reviews — ultralight options will not keep you warm.
  2. Verify the sizing. “One size fits all” means very different things across brands. Look for actual measurements (length and width) rather than trusting the label.
  3. Look for double-layered construction. Single-layer fleece hoodies are basically just oversized sweatshirts. The whole point of an oodie hoodie is the dual-layer insulation — flannel or plush on the outside, sherpa on the inside.
  4. Read the washing instructions. Cheap fleece can pill, shrink, or lose softness after just a few washes. Cold water wash and air dry will extend the life of any hoodie blanket, regardless of price.
  5. Factor in shipping time. AliExpress can take 10–30 days depending on your location and the shipping method. If you need your hoodie blanket before a specific date (holiday gift, winter trip), order well in advance or choose an Amazon option with Prime delivery.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Primark version of an Oodie?

The Primark version of an Oodie is called the Snuddie. It is priced at around £16 (approximately $20 USD), which is less than one-third the price of the original Oodie at £54. The Snuddie uses standard polyester sherpa fleece rather than Oodie’s proprietary ToastyTek, so the material is not quite as soft or dense — but for the price, it offers solid warmth and comfort.

Why are Oodies so comfy?

Oodies are so comfortable because they combine three design elements: an oversized fit that traps warm air around your body, a double-layer fleece construction (their proprietary ToastyTek) that insulates without feeling heavy, and a large hood that keeps your neck and head warm. The loose cut also means there is no restriction on movement, so you feel wrapped in warmth without feeling constrained.

Is an Oodie worth the money?

The original Oodie is worth the money if you prioritize premium material quality, exclusive licensed designs, and long-term durability. At ~$100, it will last multiple winters with regular use. However, if you are mainly looking for warmth and comfort and are not particular about fabric feel or brand designs, dupes from Primark, Amazon, or AliExpress can deliver 75–90% of the experience for $6–$33.

What is the closest thing to an Oodie?

The closest alternative to an Oodie depends on your budget. The Primark Snuddie (~$20) is the most widely discussed dupe with a similar oversized fit. For the best value, AliExpress oversized sherpa hoodie blankets in the $25–$33 range use high-density plush fleece that comes closest to the Oodie’s signature ToastyTek softness.

Are Oodie hoodie blankets safe to wash in a machine?

Yes, both the original Oodie and most dupes are machine washable. Use a cold water cycle with mild detergent, and either tumble dry on low heat or air dry. Avoid high heat, which can damage the fleece fibers and cause pilling. For AliExpress alternatives, check the specific product listing for care instructions as they can vary by manufacturer.

Can you get an Oodie hoodie for adults in plus sizes?

The original Oodie uses a one-size-fits-most design that accommodates a wide range of body types due to its oversized cut. Many AliExpress alternatives offer multiple size options including larger sizes, making them a good choice if you want a more specific fit. Always check the product measurements before ordering.

How long does AliExpress shipping take for hoodie blankets?

Standard AliExpress shipping typically takes 10–30 business days depending on your location and the seller’s warehouse. Some listings offer expedited shipping (7–15 days) for an additional fee. If you are in the US, look for sellers with US warehouse options, which can cut delivery time to 5–10 days.
